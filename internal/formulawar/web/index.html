Rules & equation reference

Use the x coordinates on the grid. Your team is always shown on the left, firing toward increasing x. The constant adjusts automatically so the curve passes through your unit. Shots pass through players and can eliminate multiple opponents. Teammates are unharmed. Hit obstacles disappear and stop that shot, opening a path for later turns. Teams alternate turns; living teammates rotate as the shooter. Last team standing wins. The match draws after 100 shots.

Take every shot yourself and clear three target dummies. Switch sides to practice firing left or right, or choose New targets for a fresh arena. Each attempt allows 100 shots, with no preview.

+ − * / ^ ( )   sin cos tan exp ln sqrt abs   x pi e

Multiplication may omit *: 3x, 3x^2, 3sin(5x). Angles are radians. Shots stop at undefined regions, any arena edge, or 600 units of arc length. Shots end when they leave the visible arena.

How to play

Enter a room name to create or join it, or choose Practice solo. In multiplayer, pick the red or blue team, and let the host start the match. You can also stay in the room as a spectator.

On your turn, write a function of x. Your projectile follows f(x) + c using the arena grid; c is chosen so the curve passes through your unit. Fire to reveal your curve. Your team appears on the negative side and fires left to right. Blue players see a mirrored board; their equations are mapped to the shared arena by substituting −x for x.

Eliminate the other team to win. Each shot has 600 units of travel, and stops at the first obstacle, undefined region, or arena boundary, including the top and bottom.
